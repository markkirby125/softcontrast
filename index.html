The Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm

SoftContrast is an anti-halation reading palette generator leveraging the Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm (APCA) and OKLCH color science to eliminate visual degradation (irradiation blur, ghosting) for low-vision and astigmatic populations.
